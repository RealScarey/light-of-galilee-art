# Light of Galilee — game art pack

Painterly Alto's Adventure–style assets for the Light of Galilee prototype (Bethsaida level).

Generated for Sean of ScareyAI and creative director Paweł Dyllus. Concept and game are © 2026 Paweł Dyllus, Light of Galilee.

These files are working art for the Replit web prototype, not final production illustration.

## Village mid-ground (v2)

`village-mid.webp` was replaced so nearby houses sit at true human scale:
doorways taller than a standing adult, houses about two to three times human
height, with more distant houses smaller, higher, and hazier. The previous
plate is kept as `village-mid-v1.webp`.

## Audio pack

Narration and cue audio for the Light of Galilee Bethsaida prototype.

- Narrator: HeyGen Voice **Sharon - Soft & Warm** (public instant voice,
  `3d99ad74a1ca43d191d2247926488ff2`, en-AU). Gentle, warm, female,
  generated 19 November 2026.
- `audio/narration/*.mp3` — Mark 8:22–26, the plain-words line, six outcome
  lines, the partial-healing line, and “This is my beloved Son.”
- `audio/timings/*.json` — word start/end times for scroll highlighting,
  plus `audio/manifest.json`.
- `audio/sfx/` — `ambience.mp3` (loop), `tap.mp3`, `star1.mp3`, `star2.mp3`,
  `star3.mp3`, `excellent.mp3`. 44.1 kHz mono MP3 for iOS Safari.

All files are served as local assets at runtime; do not depend on GitHub
from the live game after download.
